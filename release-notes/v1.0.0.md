The first public build of **Inkleaf**, a warm, hand-drawn notebook for
Android. Write, sketch, record and scan, gather notes into collections, lay
ideas out on a canvas, and find anything again in seconds. Everything stays
on your phone: no account, no ads and no analytics of its own.

This is an early release. If something feels off, please say so (see
*Feedback* below).

## What's inside

**Write and capture**
- **Notes** with titles, tags, colours and pinning. Changes save as you type.
- **Formatting:** headings, bold, italic, strike-through and lists, styled as
  you type.
- **Templates:** daily journal, meeting notes, packing list, recipe and to-do.
- **Checklists:** tick items off with a pencil strike-through. Cards show
  progress like "2/4 done".
- **Sketches:** a full-screen pad with pens, colours and an eraser.
- **Voice notes:** record with a live level meter, then play back with
  seeking.
- **Scan pages:** photograph a page or a receipt, and the text is read on
  your phone, ready to fix up and search. The photo stays with the note.
- **Share into Inkleaf:** text, links and photos shared from other apps
  become a new note.
- **Home-screen widget:** one-tap note, checklist, voice note or scan, plus
  your pinned notes.

**Organize**
- **Links between notes:** type `[[` to link another note. Each note shows
  its links and backlinks.
- **Collections:** notebooks shown as folders in a drawer. A note can live in
  several.
- **Canvas:** put sticky notes and your notes on a board, connect them with
  arrows and draw around them, with pan, zoom, undo and redo.
- **Reminders:** a notification at the time you pick, even with the app
  closed.

**Find it again**
- **Search:** covers titles, text, checklist items, tags (`#travel`) and the
  words in scanned pages, with matches highlighted.

**Keep it safe**
- **App lock:** fingerprint, face or PIN.
- **Backup and restore:** everything, including recordings and scans, in one
  `.zip`. Restoring never overwrites newer work.
- **Trash and undo:** deleted notes go to a trash you can restore from, and
  every delete has a quick undo.
- **Night theme,** or follow your system's setting.

A fresh install opens with a few starter notes and a small canvas that show
you around. They're ordinary notes, so you can delete them.

## Known issues

- **Keyboard covers tools:** while the keyboard is open, it hides the
  formatting toolbar and the `[[` link suggestions.
- **Renaming breaks links:** renaming a note breaks `[[links]]` that point to
  it.
- **Slow canvas strokes:** very slow pen strokes on the canvas can be lost.
- **Raw formatting in search:** search results show formatting marks such as
  `**` in the preview text.

## Privacy

Your notes, recordings and scans are stored only on your phone. Text
recognition runs on the device. Google's ML Kit, which does the recognition,
sends Google basic diagnostics but never your notes, photos or text. Read
the full [privacy policy](https://qharny.github.io/InkLeaf/privacy.html).
