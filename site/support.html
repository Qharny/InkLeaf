Support

Answers to common questions. Stuck on something else, or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub.

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your phone, in Inkleaf's private storage. There's no account and no cloud sync, so notes don't appear on your other devices.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone?

Go to Settings → Export a backup. You'll get one .zip with your notes, canvases, collections, recordings and scans; save it to your files, cloud drive or email. On the new phone, install Inkleaf and choose Settings → Restore from a backup. Restoring adds what's missing and keeps the newer copy of anything changed in both places. It never deletes anything.

I deleted a note by mistake

Deleted notes go to the trash first. Open Settings → Trash and tap the note to restore it.

My reminder arrived late

On Android, on-the-minute reminders need the Alarms & reminders permission (Android Settings → Apps → Inkleaf). Without it, Android may deliver reminders a few minutes late to save battery. Also check that notifications are allowed for Inkleaf.

Scanning didn't pick up my text

Scanning reads Latin-script text (English, French, Spanish, German and similar). Use good light, hold the phone flat over the page, and keep the text in focus. The photo is kept with the note, and you can fix up the text by hand.

How does the app lock work?

Turn it on in Settings → Lock Inkleaf. Inkleaf then asks for your phone's fingerprint, face, or screen-lock PIN or pattern when it opens. Your phone has to have a screen lock set. If you can unlock your phone, you can unlock Inkleaf.

Can I share things into Inkleaf?

On Android, share text, links or images from another app and pick Inkleaf to save them as a new note. Sharing into Inkleaf isn't available on iPhone yet.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstall the app. Because everything is stored only on your phone, that removes it all. Empty the trash first if you just want to clear deleted notes.
