Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Inkleaf is a notebook app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own. Your notes stay on your phone.

What Inkleaf keeps, and where

Your notes, checklists, sketches, canvases, collections, voice recordings, scanned pages, reminders and settings are saved only in Inkleaf's private storage on your device. The developer never receives them, and there is no Inkleaf server. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you've turned on your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone), that backup may include Inkleaf's data. It's handled by Google or Apple under your account and their privacy policies.

Permissions

Inkleaf asks for each permission only when you first use the feature that needs it, and you can say no and keep using the rest of the app.

  • Microphone: used only while you record a voice note.
  • Camera and photos: used only when you scan a page or pick a photo to scan.
  • Notifications: used to show the reminders you set.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android): lets reminders arrive on the minute.
  • Fingerprint or face: used for the optional app lock. Your phone checks it; Inkleaf never sees your fingerprint or face data.

Text recognition (Google ML Kit)

Inkleaf uses Google's ML Kit to read the text in pages you scan. Recognition runs entirely on your device. Your photos and the recognised text are never sent anywhere.

ML Kit itself sends Google a small amount of diagnostic information so Google can maintain and improve it: details such as the device model, operating system and app version, an identifier for this installation of the app, which ML Kit features were used, and error and performance figures. It doesn't include your notes, photos, recordings or any text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

Sharing and backups

Inkleaf only sends something out of the app when you ask it to: sharing a note, or exporting a backup .zip through your phone's share sheet. It goes wherever you choose to send it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads its fonts from this site rather than a third party. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Inkleaf isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the app, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
